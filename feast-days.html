Rank key

  • Solemnity — highest rank (Our Lady of Good Remedy, Oct 8; St. John de Matha, Dec 17).
  • Feast — St. Agnes (Jan 28); St. John Baptist of the Conception (Feb 14); St. Michael of the Saints (Jun 8); Most Holy Redeemer (Oct 23); All Saints of the Order (Nov 13).
  • Memorial — the remaining fixed memorials.
  • Optional Memorial — may be celebrated or omitted at choice.
  • Commemoration — Nov 14, prayers for the deceased of the Order.

Source: Order of the Most Holy Trinity — Curia Generalizia, official liturgical calendar (trinitari.org/calendario-liturgico-feste), cross-checked with the Order’s English Liturgy of the Hours — Proper of the Order. The Trinitarian Way handbook reproduces this same calendar.
